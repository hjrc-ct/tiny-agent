Camunda Version supported

Whats involved in the hands on labs?
C8 Labs course includes 15+ Guided Execution Steps. Once completed you will see 15 pods running the orchestration cluster including application monitoring with prometheus and grafana.

Udemy practice exam
C8 Labs also has Udemy Practice Exam to validate your Camunda 8.9 Self-Managed knowledge after completing the hands-on labs. Exam includes 70 Questions on Architecture, Operations and Troubleshooting.

Get Started
Free Tier – During Community Events. Start your 6-hour hands-on lab. Free Tier is applicable during select time intervals - such as Community Events. No waiting, no manual approval. Use the Sign In button and verify your email with a one-time password (OTP).
Premium Tier – Online purchase available. 48-hours duration. Web portal navigates the user with instructions for payment. After making the payment, in a matter of seconds, you get instant access to the learning platform. Use the Sign In button and Google as Identity Provider.
